# Brand and visual identity

Zhiyin looks like ink on dark paper. The app, the website and the videos share
one look, which comes from the name.

## The name

知音 (*zhīyīn*) comes from the story of Bo Ya and Zhong Ziqi. When Bo Ya's
music turned to high mountains, Zhong Ziqi heard the mountains; when it turned
to flowing water, he heard the river. The word means a friend who understands
what you mean. Mountains, water, mist and clouds are therefore the brand's
whole vocabulary of images. Nothing else is drawn for decoration.

## Mark and wordmark

- **The mark** is a single calligraphic Z painted with a brush. Its stroke
  crosses itself in the middle, leaving two thin slits; they are part of the
  mark. It is drawn in the ink colour on the dark ground, never boxed or
  badged.
- **The wordmark** is ZHIYIN in capitals, widely tracked (about 0.3 em), in
  the display face at weight 600, centred under the mark. Beneath it, smaller:
  知音 in a brush-style Kai face, then "one who knows the tune".
- There are no seals, stamps or red badges anywhere.

## Ground, ink and colour

- **The ground** is warm, dark paper (`#1b1918`) with a fine grain. Everything
  rests on it.
- **The ink** is pale (`#e8e2d6`): pale ink on dark paper, the reverse of a
  scroll. Text is a warm white (`#f1ece6`), with softer and muted tones for
  what is secondary.
- **Red** is the app's accent for what a person acts on: the primary button,
  Allow, Download. It is never ornament: no red headings, no red halves of
  phrases, no red marks or seals.
- A light theme exists in the app for those who choose it; the brand itself is
  shown on the dark ground.

## Type

The display face is Bahnschrift, for titles, labels and the wordmark. The body
face is Segoe UI Variable, and data is set in Cascadia Mono. Chinese is set in
a Kai (brush) face. Headings say plainly what follows; they carry no slogan.

## Ink drawing

Anything drawn is painted the way ink behaves, never imitated with geometric
shapes:

- A brush is a bundle of bristles. A stroke lands and lifts with a taper, runs
  dry along its length, and breaks into the tracks of its dry bristles.
- Wet ink spreads into the paper along its fibres, gathers at the drying edge
  and dries darker there. Washes are mostly water and stay translucent.
- Mountains are a ridge line, then washes falling down the shaded flanks,
  thinning before the foot so mist shows below. Distant ranges are paler and
  finer. Mist is paper the brush never touched.
- Water is drawn as a few wavy lines of the brush. A drop's rings are broken
  arcs, never whole circles.
- No glows, halos, gradients standing in for mist, or perfect shapes.

These pictures are painted by a small simulation of wet ink on paper, stroke
by stroke, so the same pictures can appear as stills, as drawings in motion,
and as masks for transitions.

## Motion

- **Ink moves like ink.** Drawings are shown a drawing at a time, 12 to 15 a
  second, as hand-drawn animation is, never as a smooth vector tween.
- **One thing at a time.** A drop falls, its rings spread and fade, and only
  then is the river drawn from where it fell; then the mountains; then the
  name.
- **Transitions are painted.** A broad brush sweeps across, or ink blooms from
  a point, and the next picture shows through the ink. A transition always
  closes completely, so the old picture never shows through the new one.
- **Recordings of the app rest on the ground like mounted paintings**, with a
  label on the mount below: what is happening on the left, and the model or a
  short note on the right. Each new label is brushed on, so the change is seen.
- Motion is calm and follows the music's bars; a cut lands on a beat. With
  reduced motion, pictures appear finished and transitions become cuts.

## Sound

The music belongs to the same world: mountains, rivers and clouds. It is
calm, with changes of pace, played on recorded acoustic instruments (piano,
harp, strings, hand percussion) rather than synthesized sound, and mixed so
each part stays clear.

## What to avoid

- Seals, stamps and red badges.
- Halos, glows and perfect circles posing as ink.
- Promotional or invented imagery: what is shown of the app is the real app,
  and every claim beside it is true.
- Strings, instruments or other literal props from the story; the story is
  told by the landscape alone.
