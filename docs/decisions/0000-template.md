# NNNN. Title (short, decision-focused, not a topic name)

Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What situation forced this decision. What constraints were non-negotiable
(a deadline, a platform limit, a prior incident). Only what's needed to
understand *why a decision was needed at all* — not a design doc.

## Decision

The choice, stated as a sentence, not a list of options. If alternatives
were seriously considered, name them in one line each and say why they
lost — don't reconstruct the whole debate.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it forecloses.
Include the downside honestly — a decision with no listed cost reads as
unreviewed, not as a good decision.
