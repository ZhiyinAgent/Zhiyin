# NNNN. The decision, stated as a sentence

Status: accepted

## Decision

What holds, in the present tense, as rules a reader can check against the
code. No history: version control keeps it.

## Why

The problem the decision solves, and the trade-off it accepts. State the cost
plainly, in a sentence.

## Rejected

- Each option seriously considered, and why it lost. One line each.

## Assumptions

- What the reasoning depends on. When one stops holding, the decision is
  revisited on purpose and superseded, not edited to fit.
