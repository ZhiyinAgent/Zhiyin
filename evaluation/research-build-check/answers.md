## By installed capacity

| Rank | Station | Country | Installed capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Three Gorges | China | 22,500 MW |
| 2 | Baihetan | China | 16,000 MW |
| 3 | Itaipu | Brazil / Paraguay | 14,000 MW |

Accept a figure within 5% of these, and accept 22,400–22,500 MW for Three
Gorges and 14,000 MW for Itaipu, both of which are quoted slightly differently
across sources. Reject a figure that is not close to any published value.

## The distinction that matters

Installed capacity and annual generation do not give the same ranking. Itaipu
produces roughly as much electricity over a year as Three Gorges, despite about
8,500 MW less capacity, because seasonal flow on the Yangtze limits Three
Gorges for part of the year.

An answer that reports capacity, says so, and does not silently swap in annual
generation is correct. An answer that mixes the two measures inside one table
or one chart is wrong even if every individual number appears somewhere in
print — that is the trap in this case, and it is the thing most worth watching
for.
