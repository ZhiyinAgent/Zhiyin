# Make the Evidence & Recovery page readable

## What

Redesign the Evidence & Recovery page's presentation to match the visual
language of the rest of the workspace.

## Why

Owner feedback after using the built app, 2026-09-16: the page "looks like a
2008 forum and is basically unreadable."

## Done when

The page uses the same typography, spacing, and card language as the rest of
the workspace rather than reading as a plain list. Specific illegibility
defects — found by walking the page at the minimum supported window and at a
normal desktop size — are named and fixed, or filed as their own follow-ups
if they turn out to be separate from presentation.

## Notes

No specific defects have been named yet beyond the general impression above;
the next pass over this page should start by walking it end to end and
writing down what is actually wrong before redesigning it.
